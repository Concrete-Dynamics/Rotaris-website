# Acceptable use policy

Concrete Dynamics UG (haftungsbeschränkt) — as of 21 August 2026

This policy forms part of the [terms and conditions](terms.md) and of the
[end-user terms](eula.md). It has two jobs: it protects the service from abuse, and it
keeps Rotaris out of the fields of application for which the EU AI Act provides special
obligations.

## 1. What Rotaris is

Rotaris is a tool for software development. It breaks development tasks down, runs
commands on your machine, changes source code and checks the result. The responses come
from third-party language models.

**Rotaris makes no decisions about people.** It does not assess anyone, select anyone
or classify anyone.

## 2. Prohibited uses

You may not use Rotaris and Rotaris Cloud

- for purposes prohibited under Art. 5 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act) — in
  particular not for social scoring, for biometric categorisation on the basis of
  sensitive attributes, for emotion recognition in the workplace or in educational
  institutions, and not for inferring the risk of criminal offences from personality
  traits;
- as part of a high-risk AI system within the meaning of Annex III to the AI Act, or of
  a product regulated under Annex I — for example in recruitment, creditworthiness
  assessment, educational assessment, law enforcement, migration management, the
  operation of critical infrastructure or in medical devices. Anyone who nevertheless
  uses Rotaris there becomes a provider within the meaning of Art. 25 of the AI Act and
  bears all the obligations attached to that role;
- to create malware, to prepare attacks on third-party systems or to circumvent
  technical protection measures — with the exception of security testing on systems for
  which you hold an engagement;
- to infringe the rights of others, in particular copyright, trade mark and personality
  rights;
- to process third parties' personal data without the legal basis required for it —
  which includes handing someone else's data holdings to a language model as task
  material;
- to circumvent credit accounting, to pass access to third parties or to resell model
  access.

## 3. Transparency about AI content

The content Rotaris produces is machine-generated. If you publish it or make it
available to third parties, it is your job to label it wherever the law requires it or
wherever recipients would otherwise be deceived. Rotaris never pretends to be a human.

## 4. Enforcement

In the event of a serious breach we may temporarily suspend access to Rotaris Cloud and
terminate the contract for cause. We will inform you of this and give you the
opportunity to comment, unless a statutory obligation prevents us from doing so.
Consumed credit is not refunded; unused credit is refunded in accordance with section 6
of the terms and conditions.

## 5. Reporting abuse

Report abuse to info@concrete-dynamics.com. Report security vulnerabilities following
the procedure in our SECURITY.md (in the repository, root directory).
